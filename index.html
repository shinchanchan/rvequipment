About the Company

Built in Chennai, Trusted on the Shop Floor

Established in 2016, RV Equipments is a proprietorship manufacturer of hydraulic stackers, industrial trolleys, and hydraulic elevators & lifts. Every unit is fabricated at our Chromepet workshop and signed off by the proprietor before it ships.

We work directly with plant managers and procurement teams — sizing equipment to your actual load, lift height and floor plan rather than pushing a catalog fit.
