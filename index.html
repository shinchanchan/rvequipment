About the company

Built in Chennai, trusted on the shop floor

We work directly with plant managers and procurement teams — sizing equipment to your actual load, lift height and floor plan rather than pushing a catalog fit.

Common questions

Before you call

Can you build to a custom capacity?

Yes. The capacities and lift heights listed are our standard configurations — most orders are adjusted to the client's actual load, floor plan and duty cycle.

What warranty applies?

A 12-month manufacturing warranty is standard across our product lines, covering defects in fabrication and assembly.

Do you deliver outside Tamil Nadu?

Yes — we have delivered to clients across 18 states. Share your pin code with the enquiry and we'll include a freight estimate in the quote.

Do you support installation on site?

We provide installation guidance and commissioning support, plus operator orientation where the site requires it.
